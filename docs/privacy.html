Website privacy notice

Last updated 2 July 2026

This notice explains how the shoutparty.com website handles your data. It covers the website only. The Shout Party Android app has its own privacy policy at sepulka.cc/shoutparty/privacy.html.

What we measure

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used and how it performs. It records page views, the referring site, and basic page-load timings, plus an approximate country (derived from your IP address) and your device type, browser and operating system (derived from your browser's user-agent). It does not build a profile of you, track you across other sites, or store your IP address to identify you.

Cookies

None. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless and stores nothing on your device, which is why this site shows no cookie-consent banner.

Legal basis

Processing rests on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in measuring and improving the website. Because the data is aggregate and not used to identify you, your interests are not overridden.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. provides the analytics as our processor. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and its Web Analytics page for details on how it handles the data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection regarding your personal data. Because this site's analytics are aggregate and cookieless we hold no record tied to you individually, but you are welcome to contact us with any question at contact@sepulka.cc.
