Game modes

Six ways to play.
None of them boring.

Whether you want classic Charades chaos, frantic 20-second sprints, or wager-it-all bluffing — there's a mode for every party.

💬

Classic

Describe the word without saying it. Pass, score, repeat.

⚡

Blitz

20 seconds per word — auto-skips on expiry. Pure speed.

🤫

One Sentence

Explain it in a single sentence. Honour system — no take-backs.

🎨

Draw & Act

Finger-draw on the card, or act it out. No talking allowed.

🔗

Chain Reaction

Every 3-correct streak adds +10 seconds. Keep the chain alive.

🎯

High Stakes

Bid 1–15 before the round. Hit your target or lose those points.

How it works

From install to shouting in 30 seconds.

1

Set up teams

Names are pre-filled from a 50-strong roster of localized team names. Tweak a color, add more teams, you're done.

2

Tune the rules

Round length, skip penalty, 1–3 laps, optional overtime, category & difficulty filters. Then pick a mode.

3

Pass the phone & shout

Beat the timer, rack up points, settle who's actually funniest. Confetti for the winner.
